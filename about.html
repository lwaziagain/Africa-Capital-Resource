About African Capital Source

African Capital Source (ACS) is a written financial news publication covering companies, markets, capital and economic policy across Africa. It includes automatically collected source stories alongside original ACS reports. Original reports can include context for investors, businesses and households.

ACS covers Southern, East, West, North and Central Africa. South Africa and the JSE are an important part of the coverage, alongside Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, Egypt, Morocco and other markets where credible source material is available.

Ownership

ACS is owned and published by Dream Afrika Group (Pty) Ltd, Johannesburg, founded by Lwazi Nongauza, whose publishing work includes Showbiz Scope, Gauteng Lifestyle Magazine, South African Lifestyle Magazine, Sandton Lifestyle Magazine and Post Express Africa.

How ACS works

ACS brings together news from selected publishers and official sources. Automated collection organises new entries into topics and links readers to the original reporting. Source stories are labelled separately from original ACS reports; collection does not independently verify a publisher’s claims.
