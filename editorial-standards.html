Editorial standards

Source stories

Entries labelled “Source story” are collected automatically from publisher feeds. Headlines remain attributed to their publisher. Excerpts appear only where reuse has been approved. These entries are not original ACS reporting or independently fact-checked analysis. Publication and collection times are shown separately; related stories can appear from more than one publisher.

ACS reporting

Every ACS report is based on identifiable sources: official statements, regulatory notices, exchange announcements, filings or reports by credited publishers. ACS does not publish anonymous allegations, invented quotations or figures it cannot trace.

Attribution

Each article ends with a sources section listing the publisher, title, original publication date, the date ACS checked it, the source type and a visible link. Where ACS relies on another publisher's reporting, it says so and names the journalist where identified.

Quotations

Quotations are used only where the exact original wording is available, are kept short, and name the speaker, their role and where the quote first appeared. ACS does not conduct interviews and never implies that it did.

Financial accuracy

ACS distinguishes between announced, proposed, agreed, approved and completed transactions, and between reported, forecast and audited figures. Calculations made by ACS are labelled as such. Where sources conflict, ACS reports the conflict or leaves the figure out.

Historical coverage

Reports on events before ACS began publishing carry a Historical coverage label, the underlying event date and the actual ACS publication date. ACS never backdates its own publication date.

Independence

Advertisers and sponsors do not influence coverage. ACS does not accept payment for positive coverage of any investment.
